Evaluation Criteria Excellent (4 pts) Good (3 pts) In Progress (2 pts) Needs Support (1 pt)
1. Mastery & Clarity Pronounces and explains grammar target flawlessly. Explains with minimal hesitation. Needs teacher assistance to articulate. Struggles with core vocabulary/grammar.
2. Logic & Procedure Applies grammatical rule correctly in context. Applies rule with minor slips. Requires guidance on structure. Incorrect application of rules.
3. Collaborative Work & Equity Actively engages partner and respects turns (Habit 5 & 6). Good teamwork with mild imbalance. Shows limited peer collaboration. Does not work well with teammates.
4. Plenary Response Capacity Answers peers' questions enthusiastically. Responds adequately when prompted. Hesitant during Q&A session. Unable to answer basic questions.
